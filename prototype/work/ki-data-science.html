KI & Data Science Entdecken

An interactive exhibition that made AI tangible for people who had never looked under its hood, with a playful visual identity to match.

The exhibition

In 2023 I conceived, designed and largely organised the interactive exhibition KI & Data Science Entdecken at Göttingen University’s Digital Creative Space.

Over two days, visitors engaged with artificial intelligence and data science through hands-on experiments on large monitors, with expert lectures and guided discussions. The aim: make the mechanics of algorithms and data-driven systems tangible and approachable for a broad audience.

Explaining AI

The real brief was translation. How does a neural network learn? What does pattern recognition actually see? Each experiment answered one such question by letting people try it themselves, then talk about it with researchers. Complex ideas, explained so that anyone could follow, without dumbing them down.

The look

I developed a bold, modular system of colourful icons. Neural networks, sound waves and pattern recognition, for example, became graphic metaphors.

The design language ran across all media, from posters and flyers to digital applications and the online version of the exhibition. A bright palette and grid-based structure balanced clarity with curiosity and lowered the entry barrier.

The text

I wrote all the texts explaining the experiments: playful and easy to grasp for newcomers, with enough substance to hold more advanced visitors. I worked on them in close collaboration with domain experts, for accuracy and an engaging voice.

Organisation

I also managed much of the organisation: public relations, space planning, coordinating the day of the event and curating the programme of keynote talks. The result presented its topics with professional rigour, yet stayed inviting, interactive and fun.
