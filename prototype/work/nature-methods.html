Nature Methods cover

Cell structures drawn soft and simple, each overlaid with a colour mask that shows how well automated segmentation worked.

The brief

An illustration for the March 2025 cover of Nature Methods, accompanying the publication “Segment Anything for Microscopy” by Archit et al. The paper explores foundation models for segmentation in high-resolution microscopy.

The picture

A stylised depiction of subcellular structures, including a mitochondrion, Golgi apparatus and other microscopic elements, merging clarity with abstraction.

Each structure is overlaid with segmented colour masks representing the varying levels of segmentation success achieved by the method. The layering shows both the power and the limitations of automated segmentation in complex biological data.

The look

To make the subject approachable, I chose a soft, pastel-inspired palette and simplified organic forms. They balance scientific relevance with visual appeal, bridging the gap between computational analysis and biological intuition.
